# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Argue about evidence, not about people.

## Specifics

This project attracts two groups who don't always talk to each other easily: clinicians and researchers who know the
literature, and owners who are frightened about a dog they love. Both belong here.

- **Disagree about the evidence freely.** Grading is a judgment call and challenging a grade is the point of the project.
  Bring the citation.
- **Don't condescend to owners.** Someone asking whether a supplement works because they saw it recommended is doing
  exactly the right thing. "Everyone knows that doesn't work" is not a useful reply; a link to the meta-analysis is.
- **Don't practice medicine in the issue tracker.** If someone posts about their own sick dog, point them to their
  veterinarian with kindness. Do not diagnose, do not suggest doses.
- **Assume good faith about motives.** Contributors who work in industry are welcome; disclosure is required, suspicion
  is not.

Harassment, personal attacks, and demeaning comments are not acceptable. That includes remarks about a person's dog,
their finances, or the choices they made before they knew better.

## Enforcement

Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the maintainers privately through GitHub. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this policy, and may ban repeat offenders.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
