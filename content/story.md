# Why I built this

Koda lived to fourteen.

Getting there took multiple TPLO surgeries, more vet visits than I can count, and a long stretch near the end where he was drinking noticeably more water than he used to. Each visit, I learned about treatment options the way most owners do, in a fifteen-minute appointment, under stress, while trying to remember what the last vet had said.

I did the research. I read everything I could find. And I still spent years feeling like I was making decisions in the dark, hearing about an option only after the window to consider it had closed.

## The part that still bothers me

Looking back, the data was there the whole time.

The increased thirst was not a quirk of an old dog on a warm day. It is one of the most reliable early signals a dog can give you, and there is a number attached to it. Roughly 100 ml per kilogram per day is the threshold where a veterinarian wants to know. I did not know to measure it. I had no idea it was something you could measure at all.

The same was true of the orthopedic decisions, the pain management, the supplements I bought for years. Randomized controlled trials existed for most of it. Some of them said the thing I was doing worked. Some of them said the thing I was doing did nothing at all. None of that was anywhere I could find it, in language I could use, at the moment I needed it.

The information was not missing. It was scattered across paywalled journals, written for clinicians, and buried under a layer of supplement marketing that is very good at looking like science.

## What this site is

It is the thing I wanted at 11pm, sitting on the kitchen floor with a dog who was slowing down, trying to work out whether what I was seeing was ordinary aging or something I should be acting on.

Two halves.

**What works.** Every treatment I could find good evidence about, graded honestly by the strength of the randomized trials behind it. Including grade D, the treatments that were properly tested and did not work. I paid for one of those for years.

**What to watch.** A monitoring framework with the specific numbers I did not know to collect. Water intake in ml per kg. Sleeping respiratory rate. Body condition score. Weight, tracked over time, on the same scale. The kinds of measurements that turn "he seems a bit off lately" into a dated trend a veterinarian can actually work with.

## What I am not claiming

I am not a veterinarian. Nothing here is advice about your dog, and this site will never tell you what to do. It has never met your animal, and the person who can examine the dog in front of them is the one whose judgment matters.

What I can do is put the evidence in one place, say plainly how strong it is, show every citation, and keep the whole thing open so that people who know more than I do can correct it in public.

I would have caught the thirst sooner. That is the entire reason this exists.
